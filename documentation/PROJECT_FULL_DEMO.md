## Part 2 — View All Requests

On the dashboard, click **My Requests** on the sidebar.

You will see a list of all your document requests. Each request shows:

- The document type (e.g. Certificate of Enrollment, Transcript of Records)
- The tracking number (e.g. EDOC-2026-000001)
- The current status (Pending, In Process, Ready for Release, etc.)

Juan already has existing requests here from previous transactions. You can click any of them to see the full details and timeline.

---

## Part 3 — Submit a New Request (COE)

Click the **New Request** button.

Fill in the form:

1. **Document Type** — Select **Certificate of Enrollment**
2. **Purpose** — Type: *Transfer to another school*
3. **Number of Copies** — Leave as 1
4. **Release Mode** — Select **Digital** (or Physical if preferred)
5. Click **Submit Request**

The system will generate a tracking number for this request (e.g. EDOC-2026-000009) and send a notification to the Registrar's office.

You will be redirected to the request detail page where you can see the status and the timeline.

At this point the status is **Pending** and the ball is now in the Registrar's court.

## Part 7 — Review and Approve

On the request detail page you will see:

- Requestor information (Juan Dela Cruz, Student)
- Document requested (Certificate of Enrollment)
- Purpose (Transfer to another school)
- The clearance task assigned to your office

Scroll down to the **My Task** section and click **Approve**.

You can add optional remarks, then confirm the action.

The system will automatically update the request status to **Ready for Release** and notify the student.
